# RimWorld Highlander

Highlander trait that randomly spawns commonly

If you kill a pawn that has the highlander trait there may be a lightning strike around you (damaged everyone but you) and to have a chance to gain the trait depending on the number of pawns with the trait.
- No pawns in colony with trait - 100% chance
- One pawn already with trait - 50% chance
- Two pawns already with trait - 25% chance
- Three pawns already with trait - 10% chance

When a highlander kills another highlander it creates a growth moment. The growth moment will be the standard Rimworld growth moment with all of the usual things.
