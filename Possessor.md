# Possessor

Pawn with possessor trait.

Ability "possession" - kills current body and takes over a new one.
If pawn dies it takes over the highest market value pawn on the map.
Possessor counter for each time you switch bodies. The higher the value to the more you can carry over from your previous body.

Gives a menu with the previous body and you can choose from:
- drain skill experience from the host
- host dies when they are out of experience
- specific level up moments that can steal
  - passions
  - traits
  - genes

- how to handle hediffs?

Psycasts and psycast ability always transfers.
